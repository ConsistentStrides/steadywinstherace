Before you begin
Medical disclaimer: SteadyWinsTheRace provides general training guidance. This is not medical advice. Before starting any training program, consult a qualified medical professional — especially if you have existing health conditions, injuries, or concerns.

If you experience chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, or unusual discomfort during training, stop immediately and seek medical attention.

By continuing you train at your own risk and assume full responsibility for your health and safety.
